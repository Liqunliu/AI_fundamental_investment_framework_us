# US Market Rules — GAAP, Regulatory & Structural Specifics

> US-specific accounting, regulatory, and market structure rules that affect qualitative analysis.
> Reference this file when analyzing US-listed companies.

---

## US GAAP Specifics

### Revenue Recognition (ASC 606)
- **Five-step model**: Identify contract → performance obligations → transaction price →
  allocate → recognize when satisfied
- **Watch for**: Bill-and-hold arrangements, variable consideration estimates,
  multiple performance obligations with aggressive allocation
- **SaaS/subscription**: Revenue recognized ratably over contract term; deferred revenue
  is a signal of future committed revenue

### Stock-Based Compensation
- **GAAP treatment**: SBC is expensed on income statement (ASC 718)
- **Cash flow treatment**: SBC is added back in operating cash flow (non-cash expense)
- **Real economic cost**: SBC dilutes existing shareholders; must assess separately
- **Adjusted earnings**: Many US tech companies report "Non-GAAP" earnings excluding SBC —
  the qualitative assessment should note this but use GAAP as baseline

### Lease Accounting (ASC 842)
- **Operating leases**: Recognized as right-of-use assets and lease liabilities on balance sheet
- **Impact**: Total assets and liabilities are larger than pre-ASC 842 for asset-light companies
- **Analysis note**: When evaluating capital intensity, distinguish between owned assets
  and right-of-use assets

### Goodwill & Intangibles (ASC 350)
- **No amortization**: Goodwill is not amortized under US GAAP (unlike some IFRS treatments)
- **Annual impairment test**: Required annually or when triggering events occur
- **Watch for**: Companies that have never impaired goodwill despite declining acquired business performance

### R&D Capitalization
- **General rule**: R&D costs are expensed as incurred under US GAAP
- **Exceptions**: Internal-use software (ASC 350-40), software sold to customers (ASC 985-20)
- **Impact**: US tech companies may appear less profitable than IFRS peers that capitalize development costs

## Regulatory Landscape

### Antitrust (FTC / DOJ)
- **FTC**: Consumer protection, merger review, competition enforcement
- **DOJ Antitrust Division**: Criminal and civil antitrust enforcement
- **Key thresholds**: Hart-Scott-Rodino filing required for acquisitions >$111.4M (2024)
- **Current focus areas**: Big Tech market power, healthcare consolidation, labor markets

### Sector-Specific Regulators
| Sector | Primary Regulator | Key Risk Areas |
|--------|------------------|----------------|
| Pharma/Biotech | FDA | Drug approval, pricing reform (IRA), patent cliffs |
| Banking/Finance | Fed, OCC, FDIC, SEC | Capital requirements (Basel III), interest rate risk |
| Telecom | FCC | Spectrum allocation, net neutrality |
| Energy | EPA, FERC, DOE | Emissions standards, renewable mandates |
| Tech | FTC, state AGs | Privacy (state laws), content moderation, AI regulation |

### Tax Policy
- **Federal corporate rate**: 21% (TCJA 2017)
- **OECD Pillar Two**: 15% global minimum tax — affects US multinationals with low-tax foreign subsidiaries
- **Qualified dividend rate**: 15% for most investors (20% for top bracket + 3.8% NIIT)
- **Buyback excise tax**: 1% tax on net stock repurchases (Inflation Reduction Act 2022)

### Trade & Geopolitical
- **Section 301 tariffs**: Various tariff rates on Chinese imports
- **Export controls**: CHIPS Act restrictions, entity list (semiconductor equipment, AI chips)
- **CFIUS**: Foreign investment review for national security
- **Supply chain risk**: Companies with significant China/Taiwan manufacturing exposure face
  concentration risk

## Market Structure Notes

### Shareholder Return Mechanisms
- **Dividends**: Quarterly payments, qualified dividend tax treatment for US individual investors
- **Buybacks**: Primary return mechanism for US tech; no tax to remaining shareholders
  (1% excise tax on company since 2023)
- **Net effect**: Total shareholder yield (dividends + buybacks) is the correct measure for US companies,
  not dividend yield alone

### Corporate Governance
- **Board independence**: NYSE/NASDAQ listing requirements mandate majority independent boards
- **Say-on-pay**: Annual advisory vote on executive compensation
- **Poison pills**: Rare but still used; adoption is a negative signal
- **Dual-class shares**: Common in tech (GOOGL, META, SNAP) — reduces minority shareholder power

### Accounting & Disclosure
- **10-K**: Annual report with audited financials and MD&A
- **10-Q**: Quarterly report with reviewed (not audited) financials
- **8-K**: Current report for material events
- **Proxy statement (DEF 14A)**: Executive compensation, board composition, related-party transactions
- **Form 4**: Insider transaction reports (filed within 2 business days)
